## Holidays and Vacations 

### Before the lession

**Question 1**: Do many tourists visit your country on vacation?

Yes. China is a glamorous country, famous for it's historical buildings, mysterious cultures, delicious foods and friendly people. So there are lot of travellers from all over the world visiting here every year. 

**Question 2**: What do most people do during their vacations in your country?

I think the worthiest thing that you must have a try in China is tasting various foods. Like Chinese Hotpot, Peking duck, fried stinky tofu and so on. By the way, the fried dishes in China are the most popular meal. And also, I think most tourists would like to experience Taiji, it can make people unwind.

**Question 3**: When do you prefer to take a vacation?

Well, it's a hard question, I think it depends on the type of activity I would do. I think autumn has the most comfortable temperature for most natural activites, such as hiking and climbing mountains. But if I plan to have a beach vocation, I must choose the summer.

**Question 4**: What do you like to do when you have a day off from work?

Well, the only one day off from work for me is absolutely precious, maybe I should just read a book and lie on the sofa to have a rest, or just walk along the river to contemplate my future. But I prefer to visit the Disney Land with my family, because my daughter has never been there, I think she must like it and I can't wait to see the smile on her face.
