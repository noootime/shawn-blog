### What is a hobby

Hobby is an activity that you are passionate about.

So there are many activities can be a hobby:

- **Indoor activities**
    - playing games
    - reading
    - watching TV/films
    - chilling out / hanging out with friends (this can also be outdoors)
- **Outdoor activities**
    - sports, such as football, skating, swimming, jogging, gardening (popular in the UK)
    - traveling
    - going shopping (this could be indoors, also could be outdoors)
- **Collecting things**
    - stamps
    - coions
    - NTFs, Non-Fungible token, a unique digital item
- **Creative activites**

### What do you do in your free time?

:bulb: **To get up to** = to do an activity  

- I get up to a lot of things.  
- I don't get up to much.  

Sometimes when you ask anotherone: what did you get up to at the weekend?  
It just means: what did you do at the weekend?

More simpler anser is (both is fine):

- I like to do yoga.
- I like doing yoga.

```
What's the different between: like to do sth & like doing sth
- When you want to express enjoy something, you can use both of them.
- When you want to express a choice or a habbit, you can use 'like to do sth'
```

:bulb: **be fond of sth**  

- I'm fond of playing video games.

:bulb: **be into sth**  

- I'm into playing video games.

:bulb: **be passionate about sth**  

- I'm passionate about playing video games.

Sometimes you are actually not passionate about one activity, but you've done it. Then you can use the phrases below:

:bulb: **to dablle in** = to do something casually, less seriously

- I like to dabble in cooking.
- I sometimes dabble in painting.
- Last week I dabble in NTFs.

And these phrases can express a humility emotion:

- I play the guitar, but I am just an aficionado.
- I play the guitar just for fun.
- I play the guitar, but I am just an amateur.

### Adverbs of Frequency

Simple Express

- I often paint.
- I regularly paint.
- I frequently paint.

Complex Express

- I paint whenever I can.
- I paint as often I can.
- I paint whenever I get a chance.

More Complex

- I don't paint as much as I would like.
- I don't get round to painting as much as I would like.

### The Tense

#### Present Simple

- I paint.
- I recently took up painting. *(To take up a hobby = to start a hobby)*
- I am new to it.

#### Past Simple

- I **started** painting a couple of years ago.
- I **decided** to **have a go at** painting. *(To have a go at sth = to try sth new, especially a hobby)*
- I **decided** to **try my hand at** painting. *(no limited to handwork, football is ok)*

#### Present Perfect Continuous

- I've been playing football **for as long as I can remember**.
- I've been painting **for donkey's years**. *(For donkey's years = for a long time)*

So, we can combine the previous tenses together to make a sentense:

> I often paint. I decided to have a go at painting because my daughter was taking classes. Yeah, so I've been painting for about 2 years now.
